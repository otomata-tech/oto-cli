## How to sign

Signing is handled automatically. When you open your first pull request on a
participating repository, a bot will comment with instructions: you reply in the
pull request thread with the sentence it gives you, and that is your signature.
It is recorded once and covers all your future contributions across the
Projects.

Participating repositories: `oto-core`, `oto-cli`, `oto-plugin`, `otomata-mcp`,
`oto-backend`, `oto-dashboard`, `france-opendata`, `o-browser`.
